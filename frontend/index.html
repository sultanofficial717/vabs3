Pearson Vineland-3 & DSM-5-TR Clinical Reference

Comprehensive guide to normative tables, domain scatter, and ASD pattern classifications.

1. Normative Lookup Methodology (Appendix B)

Autism Lens uses official Pearson Comprehensive Interview Form normative tables. Table B.1 converts subdomain raw scores (0 to maximum) to v-scale scores (Mean = 15, SD = 3). Table B.3 maps sums of v-scale scores to Domain Standard Scores (Mean = 100, SD = 15) and sum of domain scores to the Adaptive Behavior Composite (ABC).

2. DSM-5-TR ASD Pattern Engine Rules

Adaptive behavior profiles are cross-referenced with DSM-5-TR Criteria A (social communication deficits) and B (restricted/repetitive patterns). The Classic ASD profile is identified when Socialization is lowest, followed by Communication, with Daily Living relatively preserved and Motor skills intact.

3. Clinical Limitations & Ethical Safeguards

Adaptive behavior assessment measures everyday functional performance reported by informants. It does not replace direct clinical interaction (e.g. ADOS-2) or developmental history interviews. Generated reports must be reviewed and countersigned before clinical use.
